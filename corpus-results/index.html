Interpretive cautions

The frequency reports count terms in available transcripts, not positions endorsed by the speaker. Subtitle duplication, recognition errors, quotations, named authors, and uneven lecture lengths can distort frequencies. The current n-gram generator removes stop words before forming n-grams, so some reported expressions are content-word sequences rather than literal adjacent phrases. TF-IDF highlights terms distinctive within this particular corpus; it does not independently establish thematic significance. The CSV files contain no concordance passages or cross-document similarity matrix, so this interface does not fabricate either.
