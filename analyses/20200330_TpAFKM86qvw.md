**Central Thesis**

The central thesis of the lecture is that contemporary societal expectations—particularly those imposed by older generations (often referred to as “boomers”)—create a cultural tension where younger individuals are expected to act maturely and responsibly despite their developmental stage, leading to frustration and perceived immaturity in both behavior and leadership.
