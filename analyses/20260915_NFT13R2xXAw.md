**Central Thesis**

Stephen Greenblatt’s argument posits that authority deliberately generates subversion to subsequently contain it, viewing rebellion not merely as opposition but as a strategic tool for maintaining legitimacy and control over society.

**Argument Reconstruction**

1. **Authority Generates Subversion**: Power requires the visible possibility of disorder to demonstrate and reinforce its mastery over society. Without this “radical descent” from established order (e.g., patriarchal or monarchical structures), authority cannot convincingly assert its dominance.
   
2. **Use of Dissent by Authority**: Authority creates apparently radical doubts about itself, its legitimacy, and its ideas through the emergence of alternative voices that challenge official narratives. These dissenting voices expose potential weaknesses in power structures while reinforcing stability by showing how they can manage such challenges.
