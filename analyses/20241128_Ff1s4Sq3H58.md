**Central Thesis**

The central thesis of the lecture is that nuclear weapons, originally conceived as a deterrent against aggressive military action—commonly referred to as the “Mad Doctrine” or mutually assured destruction (MAD)—are failing to fulfill their intended function in contemporary geopolitical contexts such as the ongoing conflict in Ukraine. 4. **Paradoxical Situation**:  
   - The paradox arises because, while there is significant tension and conflict, no state appears willing to use nuclear weapons despite the potential for escalation. This situation challenges the notion that nuclear deterrence remains a viable strategy against conventional military aggression.
