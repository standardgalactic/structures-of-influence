**Central Thesis**

Stalin did not personally kill 20–30 million people; rather, he was responsible for policies that led to a significant portion of those deaths, primarily through state‑directed purges, forced collectivization, and the catastrophic famine (Holodomor) in Ukraine. The conventional death toll attributed to Stalin is an over‑estimation rooted in anti‑Stalinist propaganda and methodological flaws rather than empirical evidence.
