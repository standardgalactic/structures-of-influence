**Central Thesis**

Price controls—whether applied to wages, commodities, or services—are fundamentally incompatible with efficient market mechanisms; they inevitably generate shortages, distort competition, and create hidden economic inefficiencies that outweigh any short‑term stabilization benefits.

4. **Policy Implications**: The speaker argues that such price controls misdiagnose market failures by masking deeper structural issues rather than correcting them.
