**Central Thesis**

The lecture argues that winning a “culture war” requires strategic engagement on familiar ground—where one’s own community or institutional framework is already established—rather than contesting contested digital terrains such as Twitter. By operating within these known territories, groups can maintain control over narrative and perception while avoiding the pitfalls of alienation and public backlash that arise from attempting to dominate unfamiliar, high‑risk spaces.

1. **Power as Perception (Timestamp 00:02:40)**

2. **Appearing More Important (Timestamp 00:03:20)**
