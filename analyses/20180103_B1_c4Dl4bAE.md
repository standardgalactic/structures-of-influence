6. **Consequences of the Deal**: The mandated monopoly resulted in higher prices for consumers and reduced profits for tobacco farmers and merchants, leading to widespread dissatisfaction among the bazaar (merchant class) and peasants who were priced out of their habit.

7. **Clergy Fatwa and National Revolt**: A fatwa issued by a firebrand mullah effectively banned tobacco through a nationwide boycott until the Shah canceled his concession to Gerald Talbert. 8. **Shah’s Assassination (1906)**: The speaker notes that this episode played a role in the Shah being assassinated in 1906, followed by some democratic reforms and the establishment of the Iranian Parliament (Majlis). This period marked a lasting alliance between mullahs, bazaar, intellectuals, and peasantry against foreign interference.
