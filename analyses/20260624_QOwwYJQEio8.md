**Central Thesis**

Julius Evola presents the modern world as a regressive phase within a cyclical cosmic order, characterized by materialism and spiritual poverty, and advocates for a revival through the establishment of a “spiritual aristocracy” grounded in traditional virtues.

**Argument Reconstruction**

1. **Modernity as Regression**: Evola argues that contemporary civilization is not an advancement but a regression from sacred hierarchical orders to base materialism and fragmentation (00:07:07.560–00:09:32.720). This inversion of values, he claims, stems from the rise of commerce and money, which corrupts honor, contemplation, and quality.

2. **Historical Cycles**: Drawing on Hindu cosmology’s four ages—Satya Yuga (Golden Age), Treta Yuga, Dvapara Yuga, and Kali Yuga (present)—Evola maps the current decline to a period of moral decay and materialism (00:19:32.120–00:20:26.440). Each age represents an inherent flaw leading to subsequent declines.

3. **Spiritual Core vs. Ethnic Identity**: Evola asserts that one’s spiritual core is not biologically locked in; individuals can transcend racial or ethnic identity by embodying traditional virtues (00:13:43.440–00:14:15.200). This challenges the notion of inherent moral or spiritual qualities tied to ethnicity.

4. **Need for a Spiritual Aristocracy**: To counteract this decline, Evola advocates creating an “aristocracy of adepts” composed of genuinely enlightened individuals who embody traditional principles (00:21:06.320–00:22:13.920). He critiques the inadequacy of political or hereditary elites in achieving true transformation.
