**Central Thesis**

Thomas Frank argues in *The Conquest of Cool* that genuine, anti‑mainstream dissent has been neutralized and turned into a marketable commodity within capitalist societies. By examining historical trends—from the 1950s advertising revolution to contemporary consumer culture—Frank demonstrates how “cool” (authentic subversive identity) is co‑opted by business interests, transforming it from a radical stance against conformity into a form of consumption that reinforces rather than challenges the existing economic order.

**Argument Reconstruction**

1. **Capitalist Power and Containment**  
   Capitalism employs containment through commodification and consumerization to neutralize authentic dissent. By turning “cool” (the notion of being non‑conformist) into a product, capitalism strips it of its transformative potential, preventing genuine social or political change.

2. **Historical Moment: The 1950s–1960s Advertising Revolution**  
   Thomas Frank identifies this period as when business deliberately co‑opted and manufactured the concept of teenage rebellion. Advertisers created the “teenager” as a market segment, framing anti‑mainstream behavior (fashion, music) as inherently hip and trendy. This cultural framing preceded and influenced campus revolts and student movements.

3. **Identity Construction Through Advertising**  
   The stereotype of rebellious teenagers was not an organic cultural phenomenon but a product designed by advertising agencies—often referred to as the “stereotypical mad men.” This manufactured rebellion allowed businesses to sell products while appearing to support youthful dissent, thereby neutralizing any real political or social impact.

4. **Identity of the Baby‑Boomer Generation**  
   Many Baby Boomers engaged in consumerist forms of rebellion (e.g., owning Harley‑Davidson motorcycles, listening to rock music) while maintaining conventional 9‑to‑5 jobs and family roles. This duality illustrates how nonconformity is packaged as a consumable identity rather than an authentic challenge to the status quo.
